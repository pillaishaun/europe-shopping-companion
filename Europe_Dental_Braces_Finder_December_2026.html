Orthodontic travel kit for Sophia
Pack in hand luggage: orthodontic wax, interdental brushes, floss threaders, spare elastics, a small mirror and her orthodontist's contact details. For a loose bracket or rubbing wire, wax can help protect the cheek while you contact a professional. Avoid cutting or pulling wires yourself unless her orthodontist has specifically instructed you.
Message to send a clinic

Hello. We are visiting from South Africa with our daughter, who has fixed orthodontic braces fitted by her orthodontist at home. Do you accept visiting patients for urgent orthodontic problems such as a loose bracket or a wire irritating the cheek? Could you please confirm your next available appointment, opening hours, fees and whether you have an English-speaking clinician? Thank you.
